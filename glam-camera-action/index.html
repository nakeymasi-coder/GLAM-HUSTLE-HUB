What Glam Camera Action does

Glam Camera Action is a browser-based creative planning tool from Glam Hustle Hub. Build scene prompts, plan a production, organize characters, and keep the details of your project together.

Create and organize your project

  • Shape scenes and prompts with guided creative tools
  • Organize characters and a Story Bible
  • Work with planning canvases and reusable templates
  • Copy your finished text or save your work using the available browser, cloud, and Google Docs options

Google sign in and optional Google Docs saving

Google sign-in identifies your account for account-based features and cloud story storage. Where Google access is available, an optional Google Docs connection supports saving project documents to your own Google Drive.

For Google Docs saving, the app requests the limited Google Drive permission called drive.file. This permission applies to files the app creates or files you explicitly open or share with it. It does not grant access to every file in your Drive.

You may need to reconnect Google Docs after a session ends or a connection expires. You can remove the app's access from your Google Account connections.

Your workspace

Some project information is stored in your browser. Browser storage belongs to that browser and device; clearing site data can remove it. Keep a separate copy of important work using the app's available export or saving options.
