Last updated: August 30, 2026

Glam Hustle Hub respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you visit glamhustlehub.com, use GLAM Marketing Studio, or connect supported third-party services for analytics.

Information We May Collect

We may collect information you choose to provide, such as contact information, business information, or other details submitted through our website or tools. We may also receive limited technical and analytics information, such as page visits, traffic sources, search performance, and connected-account statistics.

Connected Analytics Services

GLAM Marketing Studio may allow the account owner to connect third-party services such as Google Analytics, Google Search Console, TikTok, and Pinterest for the purpose of viewing business and content performance in one dashboard.

Pinterest Data

If Pinterest is connected, Glam Hustle Hub may access Pinterest account and organic analytics information that you authorize through Pinterest's API. This may include profile-level performance, impressions, engagements, saves, clicks, and information about top-performing Pins or other content made available through the approved Pinterest API permissions.

Pinterest data is used only to display, summarize, and analyze the connected account's performance inside GLAM Marketing Studio. We do not sell Pinterest data or use it for unrelated advertising purposes.

How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve Glam Hustle Hub tools and services.
  • To display analytics and performance information requested by the account owner.
  • To identify content, traffic, and growth opportunities.
  • To maintain security, troubleshoot problems, and improve functionality.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information or connected-account analytics data. Information may be shared with service providers only when necessary to operate the website, app, hosting, analytics connections, or related functionality, or when required by law.

Data Retention and Access

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain records, comply with legal obligations, or support legitimate business operations. Connected third-party access may be revoked through the applicable third-party account settings when available.

Third-Party Services

Third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms. Your use of Google, TikTok, Pinterest, or other connected services is also governed by those providers' policies.

Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Glam Hustle Hub services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when services, technology, legal requirements, or connected integrations change. The current version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Glam Hustle Hub through the contact options provided at glamhustlehub.com.
