THE STORY SO FAR

Best Friends. Big Feelings. Bananas Everywhere.

One cozy kitchen. One fruit bowl. Two best friends who would do anything for each other — except stay out of each other’s business. Then a grape rolled in. Gigi hears everything in this kitchen, repeats it just wrong enough, and suddenly the bowl is a whole soap opera. Every episode brings a little misunderstanding, a big feeling, and a secret hiding right where nobody thought to look. The Fruit Bowl Secrets rule of friendship: ask first, accuse never, forgive fast — and never trust the grapevine.

THE THREE OF THEM

Meet the Bowl

Pippa — The Drama Queen

A pineapple with main-character energy and a heart too big for the bowl. She loves hard, accuses fast, and always — always — makes it right before dessert.

Berry — The Secret-Keeper

A strawberry who has never once told. Calm, witty, and always three steps ahead of the drama. If there is a secret in this bowl, Berry already knows it — and it is safe with her.

Gigi — The Grapevine

A grape cluster with a gold bow, glamorous lashes, and ears in every corner of the kitchen. She hears everything, repeats it just wrong enough, and is never — ever — sorry. The bowl has not known peace since she rolled in.
